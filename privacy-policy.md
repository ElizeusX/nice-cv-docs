# Privacy Policy for Nice CV - Resume Maker AI

**Last updated:** 16.8.2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nice CV - Resume Maker AI ("we," "our," "the App") handles your information.

## 1. Data We Collect

**Information you provide:**
- Resume/CV content you enter (name, work history, education, skills, photos, etc.)
- Content you scan via the camera for AI-assisted resume text recognition

**Automatically collected:**
- Device information, app usage, and crash data via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics
- Anonymous usage statistics to improve the App

**Third-party AI processing:**
- Text you submit for AI-assisted content generation/suggestions is sent to a third-party AI API provider for processing. This data is used solely to generate the requested content and is subject to that provider's own data handling practices.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- To generate, format, and export your resume/CV
- To provide AI-powered writing suggestions and content generation
- To improve app stability and performance (crash reports, analytics)
- To manage app configuration and feature availability (Firebase Remote Config)

## 3. Data Storage

- Your resume content and images are stored locally on your device.
- We do not operate our own backend server or database.
- Data sent to third-party AI services is processed per their privacy policy and is not permanently linked to your identity by us.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, which may collect data as described in their own privacy policies:
- **Firebase (Google)** — Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config
- **Gemini (Google)** — AI content generation

## 5. In-App Purchases / Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

## 6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data by uninstalling the App (removes local data) or contacting us at nice.cv.support@gmail.com . If you are in the EU/UK, you have rights under GDPR including access, correction, and deletion of your data.

## 7. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
